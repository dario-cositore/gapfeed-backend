## Competitor Verdict & Opportunity Score (04:50-05:45)
### Verdict System
- Added smart verdict above competitor list in gap detail
- Interprets competitor count vs signal count: "5 solutions exist, but 42% of signals complain about them" = high opportunity
- Color-coded: green (wide open/high opportunity), amber (moderate), red (saturated)

### Architecture Overhaul (based on external review)
- **Problem:** Old system returned 7-10 competitors for almost every gap, making everything look "saturated"
- **New pipeline (competitor_lookup.py v2):**
  1. Batch LLM filtering: ONE LLM call per gap sends all 10 DDG snippets, gets back relevance scores
  2. Complaint cross-referencing: matches validated competitor names against pain signal text (string matching)
  3. Opportunity Score: `Demand × 1/(1 + Threat)` where threat decays exponentially as complaints increase
- **Key insight:** Competitor with 5 complaints = ~8% threat. Competitor with 10+ complaints = near-zero threat
- **"5 solutions exist but everyone hates them" = HIGH opportunity**

### Opportunity Score Formula
```
T_c = relevance × exp(-0.5 × complaints)
Opportunity = Demand × 1/(1 + Threat)
```
- Tunable k_factor (0.5 default)
- Higher complaints about a competitor = lower threat = higher opportunity

### Verdict Categories
- `wide_open`: 0 valid competitors
- `high_opportunity`: competitors exist but many complaints against them
- `moderate_opportunity`: weak solutions, room for improvement
- `competitive`: strong solutions, differentiation needed
- `saturated`: many strong solutions covering the space

## Quote Dedup Bug Fix (06:00-06:40)

### Problem
Quote deduplication was broken for messages with natural colons in the text (e.g., "location: WEST VIRGINIA", "I'm look:"). The colon-based splitting approach was matching at natural text colons instead of the duplicate separator, causing:
- Duplicates not being removed (when first colon was a natural one)
- Or text being over-truncated (wrong colon matched)

### Debugging Journey
1. First attempt: check all ": " positions, keep longer version if shorter is prefix
2. Discovered `before === after` returning false even when strings appeared identical at position 100 (the "I'm look:" colon)
3. Verbose logging showed both before/after were 61 chars when displayed but `equal: false` - hidden character difference somewhere
4. Root cause: the first colon at position 76 ("location:") didn't match (different before/after), broke out of loop before reaching "I'm look:"
5. Multiple failed iterations trying different approaches

### Final Fix
Simpler approach: for each ": " after position 15, check if the text AFTER the colon starts with the beginning of the FULL text. If so, keep the text BEFORE the colon (the full un-truncated version). Also added `minCheck = 50` chars and threshold of 15 chars to avoid matching natural short colons.

**Key lesson:** Don't overcomplicate dedup. The simplest check (does after start with the beginning of the full text?) works better than trying to compare before/after lengths or prefixes.

### Result
All duplicate messages now properly deduplicated. Clean quotes in gap detail page.

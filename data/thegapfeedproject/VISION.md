# The GapFeed Thesis

## The Problem Nobody Solves

Every day millions of people publish their real frustrations with products and services. They complain on Reddit, rant on YouTube, leave 1-star reviews on G2, file GitHub Issues, post on HN.

**This data is public. It is massive. It is untapped.**

Founders build products by guessing. Investors fund based on pitches. Product teams rely on surveys where people *say* what they want instead of screaming about what they actually hate.

Surveys lie. Complaints don't.

## The Insight

When someone takes time out of their day to write a negative review, post a frustrated thread, or ask "is there anything better than X?" — they are broadcasting a **market gap**.

The louder the complaint, the bigger the opportunity.

## What GapFeed Does

1. **Collects pain signals** from Reddit, YouTube, G2, Hacker News, GitHub, and more
2. **Clusters them** into specific gaps (not vague categories — specific problems with specific products)
3. **Scores them** by pain intensity and market opportunity
4. **Publishes them** at [gapfeed.com](https://gapfeed.com) — free, public, searchable

Every gap page includes:
- Real user quotes with links to the original source
- Signal volume (how many people have this problem)
- Pain score (how severe the complaints are)
- Opportunity score (how big the market gap is)

## Why This Matters

A founder thinking about building a better project management tool can see exactly what 3,650 users hate about Notion. An investor can see that billing disputes are hurting 40+ companies at once. A product team can monitor their competitors' gaps in real time.

**GapFeed turns noise into signal. Signal into opportunity. Opportunity into action.**

## Our Philosophy

- **Open by default.** This documentation is public. Our methodology is transparent. Anyone can verify our data.
- **Free for researchers.** If you're using GapFeed for research or building something real, it's free.
- **Honest about limitations.** We document what works and what doesn't. No inflated numbers.

## Where We're Going

- **Deeper verticals** beyond SaaS — healthcare, fintech, real estate, education
- **More sources** — App Store reviews, Amazon, Trustpilot, IndieHackers
- **API access** for teams who want programmatic gap data
- **Custom research** for companies who want targeted pain analysis

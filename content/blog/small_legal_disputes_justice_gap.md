# The $600 Justice Gap: Why Small Legal Disputes are the Next Big Market

We've identified a massive, high-heat gap (Heat Score: 0.867) in the legal sector: the "Justice Gap" for disputes ranging from $600 to $13,000. These are cases too small for high-end law firms but too complex and emotionally draining for individuals to handle alone.

## The Problem: The Cost of Being Right

For most people, a $1,200 dispute with a landlord, a $3,000 car repair gone wrong, or a $5,000 contract violation is a nightmare. Hiring a lawyer costs more than the dispute is worth, and small claims court is a bureaucratic maze that requires days of missed work and endless paperwork.

### The Pain Points:
1. **The Pricing Barrier:** Traditional legal fees start at $300/hour. A 10-hour case eats the entire $3,000 claim.
2. **The "Shadow" Lawsuit:** Thousands of valid claims are abandoned every year because the process is too intimidating.
3. **Automated Rejection:** Large corporations use automated systems to reject small claims, knowing most people won't fight back.

## The Strategic Play

1. **Leveraging AI for Documentation:** The biggest hurdle in small claims is organizing evidence. AI tools can now ingest months of emails and texts to create a chronological narrative that is court-ready in seconds.
2. **The "Letter of Demand" Engine:** Often, a professionally drafted, legally sound letter of demand is enough to trigger a settlement. We're seeing a rise in automated platforms that handle this for a flat fee.
3. **Decentralized Arbitration:** There is a massive opportunity for private, online arbitration services that can resolve these $600-$13k disputes without ever stepping into a physical courtroom.

## The Opportunity for Builders

The next billion-dollar legal tech company won't be helping M&A lawyers; it will be the "TurboTax for Small Claims"—a platform that makes seeking justice as easy as filing your taxes.
